About

Researching how technology changes markets, services and customer experience

I am an Associate Professor of Marketing at Zayed University in Dubai. My work examines how emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, augmented and virtual reality, spatial computing and digital agents, change customer experience, service interactions and the way organizations and customers create value together.

My research combines academic theory with emerging technological contexts, with work published in leading journals including the Journal of Service Research, Psychology & Marketing, the International Journal of Information Management, Computers in Human Behavior and Information Technology & People. I collaborate with scholars in New Zealand, Australia, Norway, South Korea and Hong Kong.

Alongside research and teaching, I work with universities and organizations through executive education, workshops, invited talks, research collaboration and advisory projects.

EducationPh.D. in Marketing (2018) and Master of Entrepreneurship (2012), University of Otago, New Zealand

Research

Research at the intersection of marketing and emerging technology

Four connected research streams examine how technology changes the experiences customers have and the value they create with organizations.

Immersive technology

Extended reality & immersive marketing

How augmented, virtual and mixed reality and metaverse platforms change consumer experience, spatial engagement and value co-creation. This stream includes the AR Cloud framework for spatial computing in service ecosystems.

Selected applications: retail · customer experience · spatial computing

Artificial intelligence

AI, digital agents & human–machine interaction

How chatbots, intelligent virtual assistants and recommendation systems shape trust, perceived risk, authenticity and customers' intentions. Current work examines anthropomorphism in AI agents and multi-chatbot service systems.

Selected applications: customer service · virtual assistants · travel planning

Service theory

Services marketing & value co-creation

How consumers and firms co-create, and sometimes co-destroy, value across physical, digital and hybrid service encounters. Published work develops ideas such as value-in-being and circular co-creation.

Selected applications: service design · sharing economy · retail

Inclusive design

Neurodiversity & inclusive service design

An emerging stream on how digital and spatial technologies, including assistive AR, can be designed to serve neurodiverse customers. It develops frameworks for augmenting the capabilities of customers and services.

Selected applications: assistive technology · service design · cognitive cities
