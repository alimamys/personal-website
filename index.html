About

Marketing, services and the technologies in between

  • Ph.D. in Marketing, University of Otago
  • Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA)
  • Certified LEGO® SERIOUS PLAY® Facilitator
  • ILM Certified Trainer

I am an Associate Professor of Marketing in the College of Business at Zayed University in Dubai. My research examines how immersive technologies, artificial intelligence and digital service design reshape consumer experience, value co-creation and the design of inclusive service systems.

My work appears in the Journal of Service Research, Psychology & Marketing, the International Journal of Information Management, Computers in Human Behavior and Information Technology & People, among others. I collaborate with scholars in New Zealand, Australia, Norway, South Korea and Hong Kong.

Research

What I work on

Extended reality & immersive marketing

How AR, VR, mixed reality and metaverse platforms change consumer experience and value co-creation, including the AR Cloud framework for spatial computing in services.

AI, digital agents & human–machine interaction

How chatbots, virtual assistants and recommendation systems shape trust, perceived risk, authenticity and behavioral intentions.

Services marketing & value co-creation

How consumers and firms co-create and co-destroy value across physical, digital and hybrid service encounters, from value-in-being to circular co-creation.

Neurodiversity & inclusive service design

How digital and spatial technologies, including assistive AR, can be designed to serve neurodiverse customers.
