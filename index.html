About

Marketing, innovation and the technologies in between

I am an Associate Professor of Marketing at Zayed University in the United Arab Emirates, where I also serve as Department Chair of Management (Abu Dhabi & Dubai). My work sits at the intersection of marketing, innovation and entrepreneurship.

My research looks at the role of technology in the customer and organizational value-creation process: how augmented reality shapes customer experience and engagement, how immersive services can co-create — or co-destroy — value, and what the metaverse means for service design.

I publish in leading service and marketing journals, including the Journal of Service Research, Journal of Services Marketing, The Service Industries Journal and Information Technology & People.

Extended reality & the metaverse

Augmented, virtual and mixed reality in marketing; the AR Cloud and metaverse augmentation technologies in services.
